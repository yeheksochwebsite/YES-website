Safe spaces where young people grow into changemakers.

We work with children, adolescents and young people in semi-urban and rural Uttar Pradesh who face exclusion because of caste, class, gender or disability, and help them lead change in their own communities.

Our approach

Young people are not beneficiaries. They are partners in change.

  • Platforms to learn and speak

    Issue-based spaces where young people build knowledge and share lived experience.

    Access to technical and interpersonal skills that open doors to education and livelihoods.

  • Backing youth-led ideas

    Support for social-entrepreneurial innovations that young people design for their own communities.
